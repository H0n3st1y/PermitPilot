The New Hampshire Board of Barbering, Cosmetology, and Esthetics licenses individuals and shops that cut or style hair, provide manicure or pedicure services, or similar personal-care work.

A shop license is generally required in addition to individual practitioner licenses. Shops must meet sanitation, sink, and inspection standards. Nail salons often need adequate ventilation for chemical products.

This excerpt is a demonstration pointer to published board practice, not an official shop approval.
