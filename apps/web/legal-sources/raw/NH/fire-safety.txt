Status: demo excerpt for PermitPilot planning only. Confirm with the NH Division of Fire Safety and the local fire official.

Places of assembly, restaurants, and many businesses that admit the public are reviewed for exits, occupant load, and fire-protection equipment. A change of use or interior remodel can trigger fire review even when the building already exists.

Local fire officials typically inspect before occupancy. This excerpt is a demonstration pointer, not a substitute for the adopted fire code.
