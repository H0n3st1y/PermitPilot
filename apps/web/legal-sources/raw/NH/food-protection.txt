New Hampshire Department of Health and Human Services, Food Protection, licenses and inspects food service establishments, including restaurants, retail food stores that prepare food, and similar operations.

A food service license is generally required before serving meals to the public. Plan review may be required before opening or remodeling a kitchen. Handwashing sinks, refrigeration, and an approved water supply are typical inspection items.

This excerpt is a demonstration pointer to published NH food-protection practice, not a municipal permit decision.
